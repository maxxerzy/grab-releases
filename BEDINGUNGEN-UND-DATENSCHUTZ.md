**3.10 Retention and deletion.** Runs, finds and settings stay until you remove them. Deleting the app leaves this data in place at first. To remove everything, first switch off the Spotlight option (if on), remove the key for Claude under Settings › Sign-in (if stored), quit grab! and delete:

- `~/Library/Preferences/de.local.grab.plist`
- `~/Library/Caches/de.local.grab`
- `~/Library/HTTPStorages/de.local.grab`
